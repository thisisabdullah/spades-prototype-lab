Spades
Run

Five tables, two hands each. Hit the target, pick an Edge that bends the rules, and climb.

    No runs yet on this device.

    How it plays

    • You and Nadia (north) play seven-card hands of team Spades against a new pair at each table.
    • Each table is two hands. Your team needs to reach that table's target score across both.
    • Clear a table and choose one of three Edges. Edges stack for the rest of the run.
    • Miss a target and you can spend one of two retry chips on a fresh deal. Out of chips, the run ends.
    • The fifth table is The House. No nil bids for your team, and a failed bid costs double.

    What this tests

    Does a run structure with rule-bending upgrades give solo Spades players a reason to start another session? Targets were tuned on 15,000 simulated tables so a solid player clears a full run about a third of the time.

    Playtest data on this device

    Runs, tables, Edge offers and picks, retries and early exits are logged here with timestamps.
