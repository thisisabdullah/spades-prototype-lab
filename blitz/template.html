Spades
Blitz 3:00

Team Spades cut down to four hands of five tricks. A full match takes about three minutes.

4hands a match
5tricks a hand
8sto play a card

How it plays

  • You and Sami (north) play against Lina and Omar. Bots fill every seat but yours.
  • Bid how many tricks you'll take. Nil means none, for ±50.
  • Follow suit if you can. Spades are trumps and can't be led until someone breaks them.
  • Make your team's bid for 10 a trick. Extra tricks are bags, and five bags cost 50.
  • After 8 seconds your lowest legal card plays itself. Highest score after four hands wins; a tie plays one sudden-death hand.

What this tests

If a Spades match fits in three minutes, do more players hit Rematch? The control arm plays the standard 13-trick hand so the two can be compared on the same device.

Playtest data on this device

Every bid, card, auto-play, rematch and early exit is logged here with a timestamp.

Prototype by Muhammad Abdullah. Built with AI coding agents on a shared rules engine; all game logic runs on a redacted per-seat view, so it can move to an authoritative server unchanged.
